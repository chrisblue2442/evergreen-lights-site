Privacy Policy – Evergreen Lights

Last updated: November 2026

When you request an estimate from Evergreen Lights through our website or a Facebook/Instagram form, we collect your name, phone number, address and any details you share about your project.

We use this only to contact you about your estimate and service, schedule installs, repairs and takedown, and send you reminders about your lights. We may text or call you at the number you give us. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.

We don’t sell your information. We share it only with tools we use to run the business (for example our scheduling and invoicing software) and when the law requires it.
